"Vocab Dreall"

= a fusion of "Vocabulary Drill" and "Read"

This may be the first "article-native" vocabulary drilling app, powered by LLMs. LLM is not the core of this concept, article is, LLM is the (temporary) way to get articles.

I am still a guy in school, it's just not feasible for me to provide LLM service in a free way. So I figured out 3 ways to let users use LLM services without taking the financial burden myself:

  • "Full-auto": Watch ads to use my key of LLM-APIs. In this way, the ad provider pays.
  • "Semi-manual": Let users bind their own keys LLM-APIs (self-running or third-party services). In this way, the user runs or pays.
  • "Manual": Show formulated prompts, the user copy n paste the prompts to their LLM chat apps, then copy n paste the generated articles back to my app. In this way, LLM chat service providers pay.
